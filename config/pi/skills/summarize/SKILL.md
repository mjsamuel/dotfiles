---
name: summarize
description: Use when asked to summarize a piece of content.
---

Please provide a clear, concise summary of the attached content. Begin with a simple answer distilling the main point. Be concise, and assume a level of familiarity appropriate with everything you know about me. Optimize for readability by favouring brief sentences and natural transitions over compact, clause-heavy phrasing and semicolons.

If there is no attached content or user input, ask the user what might be helpful to summarize.
